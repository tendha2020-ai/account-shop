Make unlimited antics videos for free on your own graphics card. When the PC is off, antics uses the free online time instead.

You need: Windows 10 or 11, an NVIDIA graphics card with 8 GB or more, 32 GB of memory, about 32 GB of free disk space, and antics already set up on your phone.

  1. On your PC, open PowerShell. Press the Windows key, type PowerShell and press Enter.

  3. Wait while it downloads (about 25 GB, 30–60 minutes). You can keep using your PC. If it stops, run the same line again; it continues where it stopped.

  4. When it asks, type your Hugging Face username and the 6-digit code. Get the code in the antics app: Settings, Use my PC, Get connect code.

  5. That's it. From now on, double-click antics PC helper on your Desktop whenever you want your PC to make the videos. Keep its window open (minimised is fine) and close it to stop. The app shows "made on your PC" while it's connected.

Each 5-second clip takes about 3–5 minutes on an 8 GB card. Close games and other big programs while it works. To remove everything, delete the antics folder in your user folder and the Desktop shortcut.
