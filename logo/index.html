How it works

  1. Start Pick the shape, type your name, and tap a ready-made design.
  2. Art Change the main picture: a character, a badge, an icon or your own photo.
  3. Text Change the words: lettering, colour, size and effects.
  4. Stickers and Backdrop Add extra pieces and choose what sits behind.
  5. Save image Get the finished picture.

In the preview

  • Tap a piece to select it. A dashed box shows what is selected.
  • Drag it to move it. A pink line appears when it is centred.
  • Pinch with two fingers to resize and turn it, or use Smaller, Bigger and Turn in the bar that appears above the tabs.
