    Private chats with people you know

    1. Tap New chat and give it a name.
    2. Send the invite link to your friend or family (WhatsApp, SMS, email…).
    3. When they open it, you can message each other right here in the browser.

    No account needed. Messages are end-to-end encrypted with a key that only lives in the invite link, and your history is kept in this browser.
