    Good to know

    TikTok, Instagram or WhatsApp videos
    Save the video to your phone first (Share, then Save video), then choose it here.
    Finding your MP3
    On iPhone, Save puts the MP3 in the Files app under Downloads. Share lets you send it straight to an app or pick Save to Files. On Android it goes to Downloads.
    Keep it on your home screen
    In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. After the first visit it also works offline.
    Long videos
    A 3-minute video takes a few seconds. Very long videos (over half an hour) need a lot of memory and may not finish on older phones.

    MP3 encoding by LAME through lamejs (LGPL-3.0). Everything runs on your device.
